S5–S6). They are reference only: nothing here is built yet. When the demon
work starts, check each point below against the design doc and
`docs/decisions.md` before building.

## What the art sets out

- **Build.** Very thin, long-limbed and bat-like, with bat wings, a long tail
  ending in an arrowhead, and clawed hands and feet. Its skin is dark red
  and veined, and its eyes glow red. Its head is a long snout full of teeth.
- **Proportions** (as a fraction of standing height): head 0.14, torso 0.26,
  arms from shoulder to claw 0.62, legs from hip to foot 0.36, shoulder width
  0.26, hip width 0.18, wingspan 0.64, tail 0.80.
- **Scale.** About 2.30 m standing, 1.31 times a 1.75 m person, and about
  1.20 m tall when perched.
- **Poses on the sheet.** Stalking low, perched in a crouch, lunging to
  attack, and standing upright (marked "rare").

## Points to settle when the demon is built

- **Head.** The sheet gives it pointed bat ears. The headstone painting gives
  it long curved horns. The doc's final red ghost has devil horns, so horns
  may tie the two forms together. Ask the designer which head to use.
- **How much is seen.** The doc says the demon stays mostly hidden until the
  final lunge. The full model sheet is for building it, not a ruling that it
  is shown in full.
- **Low-poly treatment.** The game is PS1-style. The art is painted in
  detail, so the model will be a simplified low-poly version of it.
- **The HUD in the snatch image** (health bar, objective box and three item
  slots) is part of the illustration only. It is not a direction for the
  game's HUD.
- **The ghost in the snatch image** has a torn sheet and blush on its cheeks.
  These are not part of the ghost design built so far.
