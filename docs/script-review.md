# Happy Resting: script review

Every scripted thought, text and note in the game as it stands, generated from the game's data with `npm run export-script`. Write proposed wording in the Proposed column or under each note, and refer to lines by their IDs.

Thoughts are the subtitled lines in the protagonist's head. Reminders are the short messages shown when something is tried too early.

### Office note, Day 1

**N1-1.** Welcome, Monsignor,

**N1-2.** On behalf of everyone at St. Mary's, thank you for answering our request for assistance from the Vatican.

**N1-3.** As you're probably aware, we've been dealing with an increasing number of restless spirits and strange possessions. It's gotten so bad that we've had to install a drop box by the front gate just to keep up with all the possessed items turning up around the countryside.

**N1-4.** I'm sure a man of your experience is accustomed to this sort of thing, but we're just a small congregation with little training in these matters.

**N1-5.** I've taken the liberty of preparing your first case.

**N1-6.** Mrs. Kettle brought in an antique mantel clock that's been chiming nonstop for four days. We tried everything short of smashing it, but it only got louder. At one point, it even started giggling at us.

**N1-7.** Clearly possessed.

**N1-8.** I've left it on the table in the chapel, on the other side of the building. The door is locked to keep it contained, so be sure to grab the chapel key behind you. You may already be able to hear it.

**N1-9.** Once inside, prepare the ritual site. Light the four candles, stand in the circle, and give it a blessing. There should be matches and a crucifix on the shelf. I'm sure you know the rest.

**N1-10.** If all goes well (and I'm sure it will), leave the clock on my desk when you're finished. Mrs. Kettle doesn't want it back, and it seems a shame to throw away such a nice antique.

**N1-11.** — Supervisor
